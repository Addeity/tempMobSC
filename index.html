Pricing questions

Is this per user or per company?

Per company, with a seat ceiling rather than a per-seat charge. Adding the tenth person should not feel like a decision.

Who runs the server?

You do. Your hardware, your VPS, or a provider running it on your behalf. We are not in the data path.

What does the AI cost on top?

Nothing extra if you run a local model on your own hardware. If you connect a cloud model, you pay that provider directly at their rates.

Is there a free tier?

Not a permanent one. We run guided demos on your own documents so you can judge it on your material rather than a sample.

Not sure which plan fits?

Tell us how many people and how many documents. We will tell you honestly which tier is right, including if it is the cheapest one.
